# Pint Tower & Sitaware Escape

Two quick browser games. **Play:** https://cooperlad92.github.io/PintTower/

**Pint Tower**: catch the pints of the black stuff falling from the sky and stack them on your tray. Run left and right to keep the tower from toppling. Stack 10 to reach the top and drink the lot, then cartwheel off into the night.

**Sitaware Escape**: run 5 km down the road to The Curragh. Switch lanes, jump and duck to dodge Edge not Tracking, HQ down and Need Login Rights, and grab the floating pints on the way. Make it to the finish and sink a well-earned pint.

Controls: arrow keys / WASD (+ space to jump), or tap and swipe on mobile.

Plain HTML/JS, no dependencies.
